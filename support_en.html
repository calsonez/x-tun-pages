FAQ

How do I connect?

Open the app and tap "Connect". On first connection iOS asks to allow adding a VPN configuration — tap "Allow".

How much does it cost?

X-Tun is free. If you have no access yet, tapping "Connect" shows a waiting screen for a few seconds, then the VPN works for 24 hours.

The VPN won't connect or the internet stopped working

  • Turn the VPN off and on again in the app.
  • Check that the internet works without the VPN.
  • If that doesn't help, email us at support@x-tun.net.

How do I delete my data?

Email support@x-tun.net asking us to delete your data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
