Privacy Policy

Effective [[DATE]].

In short: X-Tun requires no sign-up and does not collect your name, phone number or email. To give you VPN access, the app sends our server a random device identifier and technical data. We do not keep VPN traffic logs: the sites you visit, DNS queries and traffic content are not stored.

1. Who processes the data

The X-Tun app and the VPN service it connects to are provided by [[OPERATOR]] ([[REGISTRATION]], [[ADDRESS]]), "we". This policy describes what data is processed when you use the X-Tun app for iOS.

2. Data we process

  • Device identifier. On first launch the app creates a random identifier and stores it on the device. It is not linked to your name, phone, email or Apple's advertising identifier. Our server uses it to recognise the device and issue its VPN profile.
  • Apple DeviceCheck token. Used so that the free trial is granted to a device only once. The token is verified with Apple and contains no personal data.
  • Technical request data: app version, platform (iOS), and the IP address and client information (User-Agent) of requests to our server. Used to operate the service, prevent abuse and investigate failures.
  • VPN profile data: the keys and address needed to establish the VPN connection, and the access expiry date. The profile is created on our server and delivered to the device.
  • Activation link. If you open an activation link we sent you, the one-time code from that link is sent to our server to bind the device to your profile.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, browsing or app history, traffic content, or advertising-tracking data. The app does not use App Tracking Transparency tracking or the advertising identifier (IDFA).

3. VPN traffic

Traffic between your device and the VPN server is encrypted. We do not log VPN traffic: we do not store the sites you visit, DNS queries or the content of transmitted data.

4. Where data is stored

On the device, the identifier, VPN profile and access token are stored in the iOS Keychain. Our server stores the device identifier, VPN profile data, access expiry and technical request logs.

5. Purposes

  • providing VPN access, including the trial period;
  • protecting free access from abuse;
  • keeping the service stable and providing support;
  • complying with the law.

6. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell data. Data is shared only with:

  • Apple — the DeviceCheck token, to verify the trial;
  • authorities, where expressly required by law.

The app does not send data to analytics, advertising or tracking services.

7. Retention

Data is kept while you use the service and deleted after your access ends, within the periods needed to operate the service and meet legal requirements. Technical logs are kept for a limited time and deleted regularly.

8. Your rights

You can request information about the data linked to your device, its correction or deletion, and withdraw your consent. Email [[EMAIL]]. Deleting the app stops any new data from being sent.

9. Children

The service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
